## How the Rental Process Works

**Booking Process**:
- Book online via roadsurfer website
- Select pickup station, travel dates, and camper model
- Add extras like equipment or insurance if needed
- Confirm booking and pay in customer account
- Receive confirmation email after payment

**Pickup Process**:
- Complete online check-in before pickup
- Bring required documents: valid driver's license, passport/ID for all registered drivers, credit/debit card for deposit

- Free second driver included
- Basic camping equipment included
- Flexible booking and cancellation with roadsurfer Flex option

**Insurance**:
- Standard insurance coverage included
- Additional insurance options available during booking
- Covers specific damage scenarios (details vary by location)

## Insurance & Protection Packages

**What protection packages are available?**
Three packages are offered:
- **Basic**: Affordable liability coverage with standard deductibles
- **Plus**: Reduced deductible to €800 per damage
- **Max**: Lowest deductible at €150 per damage, plus zero-deductible coverage for common damages (tires, windshield, break-in damage)

**Recommendation**: Max package strongly recommended for worry-free travel.

**What's included in all packages?**
- Unlimited miles
- Mobility service
- Breakdown protection

**How many drivers are allowed per package?**
- Basic: 2 drivers
- Plus: 3 drivers
- Max: Unlimited drivers

**What damages are covered up to the deductible?**
- Weather damage
- Underbody/body damage
- Interior damage
- Windshield damage
- Tire damage
- Break-in, theft, or vandalism damage

**What damages require full customer payment?**
- Gross negligence or intentional damage
- Awning damage
- Roof damage
- Improper filling of water/fuel tanks
- Driving under influence of alcohol/drugs
- Driving in unauthorized countries
- Driving off paved roads

**What about liability coverage (US rentals)?**
- Basic: Minimum state-required liability only
- Plus/Max: Additional €2,000,000 liability coverage included

**What specific situations void coverage?**
- Negligent driving (DUI, unauthorized driver, violating local laws, unpaved roads, size-restricted areas)
- Improper utility use (unsecured awning/roof tent, wrong tank filling, unsecured gas cylinder)
- Ignoring operating instructions causing equipment damage
- Unauthorized vehicle alterations or tampering
- Extensive preventable damage (awning/roof left open in severe weather, parking in hazardous areas, not using required snow chains)
- Ferry or motorail transport damage
- Damage in prohibited driving locations
- Gross negligence or wrongful intent
- Personal belongings stolen during break-in (only roadsurfer property is covered)

## Flex Option

**What is the Flex option?**
An add-on that provides more flexible payment, rebooking, and cancellation options for your rental.

**Important notes:**
- Must be selected at initial booking (cannot be added later)
- Fee varies by booking country

**How does Flex payment work?**
- Booking 60+ days before pickup: Pay 50% at booking, remaining 50% due 60 days before pickup
- Booking less than 60 days before pickup: Pay 100% at time of booking

**What are the rebooking rules with Flex?**
- Up to 14 days before pickup: Change/postpone/extend/shorten booking; pay any price increase or receive refund for price reduction
- 14 days up to pickup: Change/postpone/extend/shorten booking; pay any price increase but no refund for price reductions

**What are the cancellation rules with Flex?**
- Up to 21 days before pickup: 100% refund including Flex fee and travel preparation fee (booking fee is non-refundable as it's the platform usage fee)
- 21 days up to pickup: No refund except travel preparation fee (refunded up to 48 hours before pickup, as vehicle preparation hasn't begun yet)

## Rental FAQs

**Which driver's license do I need to drive a camper?**
A Class B (or Class 3 in some countries) license is required for vehicles up to 3.5 tons. Provisional or temporary licenses may be accepted with additional documentation. An international driver's license may be required in some countries. 

**How old do I need to be to rent a roadsurfer camper?** 
Typically 18â€“21 years old, with 1â€“2 years of driving experience, depending on the country.
- Europe (excluding UK & Ireland): 18 years old, 1 year experience
- UK: 21 years old, 2 years experience
- USA, Canada, Ireland: 21 years old, 1 year experience

**What documents do I need for pick-up?**
Valid driver's license, passport or ID for all registered drivers, and a credit or debit card for the deposit. All documents must be shown in original form - photos are not accepted.

**How does the RV rental security deposit work?**
At pick-up, you provide a deposit to cover potential damage or additional costs. The amount depends on your pick-up location and is paid in local currency by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express in USA). The deposit is held for up to 30 days and automatically released when the vehicle is returned in perfect condition.

**Can I travel to multiple countries with my rental?**
Most EU and Schengen countries are allowed. Some non-EU destinations depend on insurance and pick-up location. Travel is allowed across the United States and Canada, but not to Mexico. Certain areas, such as Death Valley, have restrictions.

**What equipment is included in my camper?**
Every roadsurfer campervan comes with essential camping equipment: camping table and chairs, leveling ramps, power cable, water canister, air conditioning (not stationary), and heating.

**How do I cancel or modify my booking?**
Bookings can be modified up until shortly before your selected pick-up window via your customer account.

**What should I do if I have an accident or if my vehicle breaks down?**
First, get to safety, check the condition of everyone involved, and call roadside assistance or emergency services. Document the situation with photos and complete accident reports if necessary. Send details to roadsurfer via the contact form.

**How does the RV return process work?**
Follow the instructions in the provided return checklist (available in your customer account 2 days before return), arrive on time at the return location, and complete the handover including inspection. Clean the windshield and bumper of heavy dirt - no car wash needed. Empty the gray water tank and toilet, refuel, and top up all necessary fluids before return.

**Where can I refuel my camper before returning it?**
You can refuel at nearby gas stations before returning your camper. Gas stations are conveniently located near every return location.
